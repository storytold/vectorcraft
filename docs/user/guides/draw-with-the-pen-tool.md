---
title: Draw a path with the Pen Tool
description: Draw straight and curved paths with the Pen Tool, close or end them, and adjust the anchor points with the Direct Selection Tool.
order: 20
verified: 0.10.0
---

Pick the **Pen Tool** (shortcut P), click for a corner point, click and drag for a curve, and click the first point to close the path.

## Draw a path

1. Pick the **Pen Tool** in the toolbar, or press P.
2. Click on the artboard to place a corner point.
3. Click somewhere else to place the next point. You get a straight segment. To place a smooth point, click and drag instead. The drag pulls out two matching handles that shape the curve.
4. Hold Shift while you place a point to keep it at a 45 degree step.
5. To close the path, press on the first point. If you drag as you press, the drag shapes the closing curve.
6. To leave the path open, press Enter or Esc, or pick another tool.

While you draw, a preview line shows where the next segment will go. This is the rubber band preview. The preference is **Enable Rubber Band for** › **Pen Tool**, in **Edit** › **Preferences…** under **Selection & Anchor Display** (on macOS, **VectorCraft** › **Settings** › **Selection & Anchor Display…**).

## Continue or join an open path

- Click an end point of an open path to continue it.
- While you draw, click an end point of another open path to join the two into one path.

## Adjust the points afterwards

1. Pick the **Direct Selection Tool** (shortcut A).
2. Click an anchor point to select it. Hold Shift to add more points.
3. Drag the point to move it. Drag a handle to reshape the curve.

## Add and delete points

With a path selected, the Pen Tool adds a point where you click on a segment, and deletes a point where you click on one. To start a new path on top of a selected path instead, hold Shift while you click. The preference **Disable Auto Add/Delete**, under **General** (on macOS, **VectorCraft** › **Settings** › **General…**), turns the add and delete behavior off. The toolbar group of the Pen Tool also has the **Add Anchor Point Tool** (+), the **Delete Anchor Point Tool** (-) and the **Anchor Point Tool** (Shift+C).
